About Neucent

A software team that treats your product like its own

Neucent Technologies builds web applications, mobile apps and AI-powered software for businesses that want a partner, not just a vendor.

Who we are

Engineers, designers and problem-solvers

We are a software development company focused on building products that people rely on every day. Our work spans web platforms, mobile apps and the growing set of AI capabilities that make software more helpful.

We care about the unglamorous parts as much as the visible ones: clear requirements, tidy code, honest estimates, tests that run on every change and documentation your team can actually use.

Our approach to AI is the same as our approach to everything else. Start from a real problem, test the idea early, and ship it with the monitoring and safeguards it needs to be trusted.

Our story

From two people to clients across Africa, India and the USA

  1. 2020

    Neucent begins

    We start as a two-person company: one founder in the USA and one in India, building web and mobile products for early clients.

  2. 2023

    Growing across continents

    Customers in Zimbabwe, the rest of Africa and the USA join us, and the work expands into products such as a family-history platform, healthcare and telecom software for US clients, and a social platform.

  3. In India

    Rooted in India too

    From our Noida office we build for Indian clients as well, including a welfare society in Bijnor and a school with its own management system, and we are growing our work in India, Africa and the USA.

  4. Today

    Built around AI

    We combine web, mobile and cloud engineering with AI features such as chatbots, voicebots and automation.

What guides us

How we choose to work

Direct access to the people building

You talk to the engineers and designers doing the work, not through layers of account managers.

A demo every cycle

Progress is something you can click through, not a status report you have to trust.

You own the code

Quality and security by default
