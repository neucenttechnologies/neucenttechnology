Questions

Things people ask us

Do you have an office in Tanzania?

Yes. Our Tanzania office is at Saba Saba Apartment, Gran Melia, Arusha. For any enquiry you can also write to info@neucenttechnologies.com or use the contact form.

Which cities in Tanzania do you serve?

We work with businesses in Arusha, Dar es Salaam, Dodoma, Mwanza, Zanzibar, Mbeya, Moshi and across Tanzania. Projects run over video calls and shared task boards, with in-person meetings where they help.

Can you build a mobile app or website for my business in Tanzania?

Yes. Tell us what you want to build and we reply with questions, ideas and a suggested first step, including a plan and a price range you can review before committing.

Do you build school software for Tanzanian schools?

Yes. Our school management system covers admissions, attendance, fees, staff records, timetables and notices, with SMS or WhatsApp alerts. We adapt it to your school's classes and terms.
