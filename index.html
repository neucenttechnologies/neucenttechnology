How we work

A clear path from first call to live product

  1. 01

    Discover

    We learn your users, goals and constraints, then agree on scope, priorities and a realistic plan.

  2. 02

    Design

    Wireframes and clickable prototypes let you react to something real before code is written.

  3. 03

    Build

    Short agile cycles with a working demo at the end of each one, so progress is visible and course corrections are cheap.

  4. 04

    Launch

    Testing, security checks and a staged release, with documentation and a handover session for your team.

  5. 05

    Improve

    We stay on to fix, monitor and extend the product based on how people really use it.

Questions

Things people ask before we start

How long does a project take and what does it cost?

It depends on scope. A focused prototype can take a few weeks, while a full product takes months. After a short discovery call we share a plan with timelines and a price range you can review before committing.

Can you add AI to an application we already have?

Yes. Most AI work we do is adding one specific capability, such as a chatbot, search, summaries or document reading, to an existing web or mobile product through its current backend.

Will you work with our existing codebase or team?

Yes. We can take over a codebase, extend it, or work alongside your developers. We begin with a review so estimates are based on what is really there.

Can we hire developers for a specific role?

Yes. You can bring in Android, iOS, React, Node.js, PHP, WordPress or DevOps engineers on a dedicated basis and manage the work alongside your own team.

Who owns the code and the designs?

You do. Source code, design files and cloud accounts are set up in your name and handed over with documentation.

How do you handle sensitive data in AI projects?

We agree on data handling before any work starts. Depending on your requirements we can use hosted models with suitable terms or deploy inside your own cloud account.

What happens after launch?

We can keep supporting the product with monitoring, fixes and new features under a support retainer, or hand it fully to your team.

Have a product in mind?

Tell us what you want to build. We will reply within one working day with questions, ideas and next steps.
