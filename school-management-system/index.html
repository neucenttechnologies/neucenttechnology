School software

School website and management system

One place to run admissions, attendance, fees, teachers, classes and notices, together with a public website that shows your school at its best.

Overview

Built around how a school really runs

A school keeps track of a great deal: who is enrolled, who came in today, what each family owes, which teacher takes which class. Our school management system gathers that information in one place, next to a public website that keeps parents and the community informed.

We have built it for Jain Vidhya Mandir Inter College in Nehtaur, Bijnor, Uttar Pradesh, a school established in 1948 that teaches classes 6 to 12. Read the case study.

The management system

What it covers

Seven modules for the daily work of students, teachers and the school office.

Students

Everything about a student, from the first admission to daily attendance and fees.

Admission and student records

Handle admissions and keep a profile for every student, with personal details and class information in one record.

Attendance

Mark attendance every day and see attendance reports for students.

Fees and payments

Set up the fee structure, see what is due, issue receipts and track payments.

Teachers and classes

Who teaches what, and when.

Teacher and staff records

Keep a profile for each teacher and staff member: subjects, assigned classes and contact details.

Timetable and classes

Set up classes, sections and subjects, and build the timetable around them.

Communication

Keeping families and staff informed without phone calls and paper notes.

Notices and circulars

Publish announcements and circulars for the school from one place.

SMS and WhatsApp alerts

Send automatic messages, for example about attendance or fees.

The public website

A clear face for the school

The website is what parents, students and the community see first. It carries the school's story and its latest notices.

  • Home page with a photo slider and a notice board
  • Messages from the principal and the manager
  • A faculty section introducing the teaching staff
  • About page with academic details, streams and facilities
  • Contact page with address, phone, email and visiting hours
