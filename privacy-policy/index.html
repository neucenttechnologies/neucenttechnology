Privacy

Privacy policy

What we collect when you use this website, why, and the choices you have. We keep it short and plain.

This policy applies to neucenttechnologies.com and the enquiries you send us through it. The website is run by Neucent Technologies Pvt Ltd, C-319, 3rd Floor, Noida One, Rasoolpur Nawada, IT Park, Sector 62, Noida, Uttar Pradesh 201309.

What we collect

We collect only what you choose to send us.

  • Contact form: your name, email address, phone number (optional), the service you are interested in, a budget range and your message.
  • Email, phone and WhatsApp: whatever you share when you contact us directly, such as your name, number and the details of your project.

What we do not collect

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. It does not load fonts, scripts or embedded content from other companies.

Like any web host, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (for example your IP address and the pages requested) for security and reliability.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and to discuss or deliver the services you ask about.
  • To keep a record of our communication with you.
  • To protect the form from spam. For this we briefly remember a scrambled (hashed) form of your IP address and the time of your submissions, so that one visitor cannot send many messages in a short period.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is kept and who sees it

Enquiries are stored in our database and/or emailed to our team. They are seen only by people at Neucent who need them to respond. We share information with others only where it is needed to run the website or deliver your project (for example our hosting and email providers), or where the law requires it.

The WhatsApp button opens WhatsApp, which is run by another company and has its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as we need them to respond to you and to keep a record of our communication, and then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Email info@neucenttechnologies.com and we will reply.

Changes to this policy

If we change how this website handles personal information, we will update this page. Last updated: November 2026.
