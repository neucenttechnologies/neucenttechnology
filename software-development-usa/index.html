Questions

Things people ask us

Do you work with US clients?

Yes. We have delivered projects for US clients, including healthcare and telecom work, and one of our two founders is based in the USA.

How do you handle the time difference?

We schedule regular calls in overlapping hours and share progress through demos and a shared task board, so work moves forward while you sleep.

Who owns the code?

You do. Source code, design files and cloud accounts are set up in your name and handed over with documentation.

Can you add AI to our existing application?

Yes. Most AI work is adding a specific capability, such as a chatbot, search or document reading, to an existing product through its current backend.
